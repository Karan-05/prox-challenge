# Controls, LCD Menus, and Settings (manual pp.8–9, 20–21, 30–33)

## Front panel (p.8)
- **LCD Display** with **Home Button** (left) and **Back Button** (right).
- **Left Knob / Main Control Knob / Right Knob** below the screen (the Control Knob is the center one; press it to select/advance, press Back to go back).
- **Power Switch** on the lower front.
- **MIG Gun / Spool Gun Cable Socket** (left), **Spool Gun Gas Outlet**, **Negative (−) socket**, **Wire Feed Power Cable**, **Positive (+) socket** along the bottom.
- **Storage Compartment** on top of the lower housing.

## Interior (behind the wire door) (p.9)
Wire Spool + Spool Knob, Wire Feed Mechanism with **Feed Tensioner**, **Idler Arm**, **Feed Roller Knob**, **Wire Inlet Liner**, **Cold Wire Feed Switch** (top), and the **Foot Pedal Socket** and **Wire Feed Control Socket** (bottom).

## Selecting a process and settings (MIG/Flux example, p.20)
1. Press **Home**. 2. Turn the Main Control Knob until the desired process shows (e.g. "MIG Stainless Steel", "MIG Steel C25", TIG, Stick). 3. Press the knob to select. (Knob press = next screen; Back = previous.)
4. Per-process screens:
   - **Polarity & gas screen** — shows which socket gets which cable and the gas type (e.g. "Stainless Tri-Mix", "100% Argon", "No Gas Required"). Plug cables per screen; set SCFH 20–30 (MIG) / 10–25 (TIG).
   - **Diameter & thickness screen** — Left Knob sets wire/rod/electrode diameter; Right Knob sets material thickness (e.g. .025" / 24Ga).
   - **Auto Weld Settings screen** — MIG/Flux: Left Knob = Wire Feed Speed (amperage), Right Knob = Voltage. TIG/Stick: Left Knob = output amperage, Right Knob = ON to energize torch/electrode holder (**warning: welder is then energized with OCV present**). The status bar shows process, gas type, wire diameter, material thickness, and the optional settings values.
   - **If you adjust WFS or Voltage manually, a white mark on the line shows the recommended setting** for your wire diameter and workpiece thickness. [p.20 note]

## Optional settings per process
- **MIG/Flux (p.21):** Run-In WFS (wire speed before wire touches work, as % of preset WFS) · Inductance (increase = more fluid puddle and flatter bead; decrease = colder puddle) · Spot Timer · Recall Setting · Save Setting (5 memory slots; press knob → OVERWRITE MEMORY SETTING → YES).
- **TIG (p.31):** Recall Setting · Save Setting (5 slots).
- **Stick (p.33):** Hot Start (amperage boost at start of weld) · Arc Force (adjusts penetration and smoothness) · Recall Setting · Save Setting (5 slots).
- Manual note: initial settings may need adjusting after inspecting a test weld — "proper welding takes experience."

## Settings chart on the machine
A **Settings Chart lives on the inside of the welder door** (referenced pp.14, 21, 26, 30: gas type, SCFH values, tungsten size for material thickness). The manual itself does not reprint a full WFS/voltage-vs-thickness matrix — the machine's Auto Weld mode computes recommended values once you enter wire diameter + thickness.

## Screen elements on the weld screen (p.20)
Process · Gas Type · Wire Diameter · Material Thickness · Run-In WFS · Inductance · Spot Timer values, plus live WFS (in/min), Amps, and Volts readouts.

## Maintenance related to the display (p.41)
LCD Screen Cover is replaceable: pry the frame off the display with a flathead screwdriver in a side slot, insert new Screen Cover (17) into Screen Frame (18) gap-down, snap frame back top-edge first.
