# Polarity and Cable Hookup — the most-asked setup question

The front panel has a **Negative (−) socket** and a **Positive (+) socket** at the bottom (plus the MIG Gun/Spool Gun cable socket and Spool Gun gas outlet). Cables insert and **twist clockwise all the way to lock**. The LCD shows the correct hookup diagram for each process during setup ("Plug cables in according to screen"). [pp.8, 13–14, 20]

## Per-process polarity map

| Process | Electrode side | In POSITIVE (+) socket | In NEGATIVE (−) socket | Gas |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MIG, solid-core wire (gas-shielded)** | DCEP — wire positive | **Wire Feed Power Cable** | Ground Clamp Cable | Yes (C25 / Tri-Mix, 20–30 SCFH) |
| **Flux-Cored, gasless wire** | DCEN — wire negative | **Ground Clamp Cable** | Wire Feed Power Cable | No |
| **TIG (DC)** | DCEN — torch negative | **Ground Clamp Cable** | TIG Torch Cable | 100% Argon, 10–25 SCFH |
| **Stick** | DCEP — electrode positive | **Electrode Holder Cable** | Ground Clamp Cable | No |

Sources: DCEN flux-cored p.13 (step 16); DCEP solid-core MIG p.14 (step 17); TIG p.24 (steps 1–2) and p.30 (polarity screen); Stick p.27 (steps 1–2) and quick-start guide page 2 (Stick/MIG/Flux/TIG cable setups).

## Why it matters
- **Wrong polarity is a listed cause of porosity, excessive spatter, and unstable arc** — the troubleshooting matrix says: ensure **DCEP for MIG** welding and **DCEN for Flux-Cored self-shielded** welding. [pp.37, 42–43]
- The "CTWD Too Long **or Wrong Polarity**" defect pattern in the wire-weld examples shows a ropey, spattery bead — the fix is "Check Polarity and maintain less than 1/2" CTWD". [p.35]

## Extra hookup notes
- **MIG/Flux:** the MIG gun's cable connector goes through the front hole into the wire feed socket (fully inserted, no O-rings exposed, or gas leaks); the wire feed **control** cable plugs into the Wire Feed Control Socket inside the machine (fits one orientation only). [p.13, steps 13–15]
- **TIG:** foot pedal (sold separately) cable goes through the front hole to the **Foot Pedal Socket inside the welder**; secure collar clockwise. Leave wire feed power disconnected. Shielding gas hose connects the TIG torch cable connector to the regulator outlet. [pp.24–25, quick-start p.2]
- **Spool gun (optional, aluminum):** ground clamp → Negative, wire feed power → Positive (DCEP), spool gun gas hose → Spool Gun Gas Outlet on the front. [p.17, p.8]
- **Ground clamp:** always clamp to bare metal on the workpiece near the weld, or to a metal bench the workpiece is clamped to. "Ground connection depends on desired welding polarity." [pp.19, 29]
- Never weld without the grounding clamp connected. TIG: a metal work bench must be grounded when TIG welding. [pp.30, 31]
