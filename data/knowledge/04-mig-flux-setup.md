# MIG / Flux-Cored Setup — wire, feed system, gas (manual pp.10–17)

**Before any setup: Power Switch OFF and unplug the welder.** Remove protective foam/cardboard before first use. [p.10]

## 1. Install the wire spool (pp.10–11)
- Open the side Door via the Door Latch.
- **1–2 lb spools:** remove wingnut and spacer → spool onto Spool Spindle against the Spool Brake Pad → replace spacer → secure with wingnut. [p.10, steps 3–5]
- **10–12 lb spools:** remove wingnut and spacer → place the **Spool Adapter** over the spindle against the brake pad → spool over the adapter, lining up the adapter pin with the hole in the spool → spacer → wingnut → screw the **Spool Knob** into the adapter. [p.11, steps 6–10]
- **The spool must unwind CLOCKWISE.** If the spool spins freely the wingnut is too loose — the wire will unravel and tangle. [pp.10–11]

## 2. Match the feed roller to the wire (p.12)
- Unscrew the Feed Roller Knob (counterclockwise), remove it, and flip or swap the Feed Roller so the **stamped number showing matches the wire diameter on the spool**.
- Solid core uses the **V-groove** roller (grooves: 0.030/0.035 and 0.025). Flux-cored uses the **knurled-groove** roller (grooves: 0.045 and 0.030/0.035).
- Screw the knob back on.

## 3. Thread the wire (p.15)
- **Hold the wire end securely under tension the whole time** or it will unspool and tangle (IMPORTANT box).
- Cut off bent/crimped wire; the end must have no burrs (cut again if needed).
- Guide at least 12" of wire into the Wire Inlet Liner and Feed Guide.
- Seat the wire in the feed-roller groove, push the Idler Arm down, swing the Feed Tensioner up to latch across the arm — only release the wire once the tensioner holds it.
- **Tension setting: 3–5 for solid wire, 2–3 for flux-cored** (too much force crushes flux-cored wire).
- Remove the nozzle (pull) and contact tip (unscrew counterclockwise) from the MIG gun; lay the gun cable out straight; leave the door open to observe.

## 4. Feed wire through the gun (p.16)
- DANGER: parts may be at welding voltage — keep hands away from the feed mechanism, close the door before plugging in **unless** using Cold Wire Feed, and never touch the trigger while feeding wire through.
- Plug the 120V or 240V cord into Power Input (only fits one way) → grounded GFCI outlet → power ON.
- Point the gun away and hold the **Cold Wire Feed Switch** (inside, above the feed mechanism) until wire feeds out ~2". If the liner comes out with the wire, just push it back into the gun.
- If wire doesn't feed and the spool is stationary: power off, unplug, slightly tighten the Feed Tensioner clockwise, retry.

## 5. Set drive tension (p.17)
- Press the trigger to feed wire against a piece of wood 2–3" away. **The wire should bend** when it hits — if it stops feeding instead, unplug and tighten the tensioner slightly, then retest. Note: wire stops feeding 3 seconds after trigger press if there's no arc, so check within 3 seconds.
- Then: power OFF, unplug, close and latch the door.

## 6. Contact tip and nozzle (p.17)
- Choose a contact tip **matching the wire size**, slide over the wire, thread clockwise into the gun, tighten. Replace the nozzle. **Cut wire to 1/2" stickout.**

## 7. Shielding gas — solid-core MIG only (pp.14, 21)
- Determine gas from the **Settings Chart inside the welder door** (typ. C25 for steel; Stainless Tri-Mix for stainless — the LCD polarity screen names the gas).
- Secure the cylinder upright to a cart/cabinet with two straps. Crack the valve briefly to blow out dust ("Briefly open valve to clean"), close it.
- Thread the included Regulator onto the cylinder, wrench-tighten. **C100 (100% CO2) needs the included CGA 580/320 adapter** between regulator and cylinder.
- Attach the Gas Hose to the regulator outlet and the welder's gas inlet; wrench-tighten both.
- To weld: open the cylinder valve **all the way**, set flow to the SCFH on the settings chart — **20–30 SCFH** for MIG. [p.20 step 4a, p.21]
- DANGER (asphyxiation): never open gas without ventilation; fix leaks immediately; shielding gas without CO2 can cause asphyxiation without shortness of breath. [p.21, p.30]
- When done: close the cylinder valve securely, remove regulator, replace cap, disconnect hose, store the cylinder secured. [p.23 step 12]

## Optional Spool Gun for aluminum (p.17)
Ground clamp → Negative, wire feed power → Positive; spool-gun cable connector into the wire feed socket; control cable to the Wire Feed Control Socket inside; spool-gun gas hose to the **Spool Gun Gas Outlet** on the front panel. See the spool gun's own manual for operation.

## Stainless wire note (p.15)
Stainless wire is stiffer and feeds harder — keep the gun cable especially straight when feeding it.
