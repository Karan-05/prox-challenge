# Quick Start Guide summary (quick-start-guide.pdf, 2 pages)

Condensed first-run flow for flux/wire welding (page 1):
1. **Connect cables** for flux wire welding — see cable setups below.
2. **Install wire spool** (2 lb shown: wingnut → spacer → spool on spindle against brake pad; adapter + knob required for 10–12 lb spools). Insert wire through **both feed guides** and clamp into the feed mechanism. See the manual to change wire size or use solid wire.
3. **Take off nozzle and contact tip**, plug the selected cord into the welder and wall, turn the welder on, and press the **cold wire feed switch** until wire comes out.
4. **Incrementally tighten the wire-feed tension knob clockwise** until the wire bends against a wood block at 2–3" (feed-tension test).
5. **Unplug the welder and replace nozzle and tip.**
6. **Hold the gun clear of the workpiece**, plug in, turn on, set weld thickness and other settings.

Warnings on the card: read the manual before use; keep torch and wire clear of grounded objects while plugged in (fire/injury).

## Cable setups (page 2)
- **STICK:** ground clamp → negative; electrode holder → positive; leave wire feed power disconnected.
- **MIG:** ground clamp → negative; wire feed power → positive; MIG gun → wire feed socket; wire feed control → inside welder.
- **FLUX:** follow MIG setup **but** ground clamp → positive and wire feed power → negative.
- **TIG:** ground clamp → positive; TIG torch → negative; gas line → gas regulator; foot pedal → inside welder; leave wire feed power disconnected.

"Basic welding instructions and welding tips are in manual."
