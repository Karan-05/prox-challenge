# Wire Welding: Operation & Technique (manual pp.18–23)

## Which wire process (p.18)
- **Flux-cored** welds mild + stainless steel **without shielding gas** — ideal outdoors/windy, forgiving on rusty/dirty steel.
- **MIG (solid wire + gas)** welds mild + stainless steel; can weld **thinner** workpieces than flux-cored; clean, minimal spatter.
- Aluminum: optional spool gun + aluminum wire + shielding gas.
- Practice on scrap whenever you change thickness, material, joint type, or process.

## Setting up the weld (p.19)
1. Practice on scrap of the **same thickness** as the real workpiece. Clean weld surfaces to **bare metal** (wire brush / angle grinder) — no rust, paint, or oil.
2. Clamp the workpieces; control any gap; **chamfer/bevel thick workpieces** for full penetration.
3. Clamp the ground cable to bare metal near the weld (or to the metal bench holding the work).
4. Power switch OFF → plug into grounded GFCI 120V(20A)/240V receptacle → set gun down on a non-conductive surface → power ON.
5. Vehicle welding notice: disconnect the vehicle battery (both terminals) first to protect its electronics.

## Making the weld (pp.22–23)
- Press and hold the trigger and touch the wire to the workpiece to ignite the arc.
- **Stringer bead** = straight steady line (narrow weld). **Weave bead** = drawing wire back and forth across the joint (wider weld; takes practice).
- **Gun angle (viewed from the front of the joint):** 90° straight up-and-down for butt (end-to-end) welds; **45° for fillet (T) welds**.
- **Travel angle:** MIG solid wire w/ gas → **push angle**, tilted 0–15° *away from* the travel direction. Flux-cored w/o gas → **drag angle**, tilted 0–15° *toward* the travel direction. (Push solid, drag flux.)
- **CTWD (contact tip to work distance): keep within 1/2"**.
- Weld a few seconds on scrap, stop, inspect against the Welding Tips diagrams (pp.34–40), adjust, and **follow the duty cycle**.
- Weld will be hot — don't touch.

## Shutting down (p.23)
Set the gun on a heat-proof non-conductive surface → power OFF → cool → unplug → remove ground clamp → disconnect gun. To respool wire: clip the wire, remove nozzle/contact tip, release the idler arm, rotate the spool counterclockwise while holding the wire end (it unravels fast once free of the feeder). MIG only: close the gas cylinder valve, remove the regulator, cap the cylinder, disconnect the hose, store secured.

## Protective gear (pp.18, 28)
Minimum **shade 10** full face shield/welding mask, ear protection, welding gloves, sleeves and apron, NIOSH-approved respirator, fire-resistant work clothes without pockets. Arc light causes permanent eye/skin damage; don't breathe fumes. Keep the welding tip clear of grounded objects whenever the unit is plugged in and on.
