# Specifications (manual p.7)

## MIG
| Spec | 120VAC / 60Hz | 240VAC / 60Hz |
|---|---|---|
| Current input at output | 20.8A at 100A | 25.5A at 200A |
| Welding current range | 30–140A | 30–220A |
| Rated duty cycle | 40% @ 100A; 100% @ 75A | 25% @ 200A; 100% @ 115A |

- Max OCV: 86 VDC. Weldable: mild steel, stainless steel, aluminum (with optional spool gun).
- Wire capacity — solid core: 0.025" / 0.030" / 0.035"; flux-cored: 0.030" / 0.035" / 0.045".
- Wire speed: 50–500 IPM. Spool capacity: up to 12 lb.

## Power requirements (pp.6, 16, 25)
- Properly grounded, **GFCI-protected** 120VAC (20A-rated) or 240VAC receptacle, with delayed-action circuit breaker or fuses.
- **No extension cords** on this welder (p.6). Use only a supplied power cord or identical replacement; never a thinner/longer cord.
- Overload protector Reset Button is on the back of the machine (pp.43, 46).
