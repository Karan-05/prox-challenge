# Stick Welding (manual pp.27–29, 32–33)

Stick welds mild steel and stainless steel with a stick electrode, **no shielding gas**. Deep penetration, forgiving of rust/dirt, good outdoors. [pp.28, selection chart]

## Cable setup (p.27, quick-start p.2)
- **Ground Clamp Cable → Negative (−)** socket, twist clockwise to lock.
- **Electrode Holder Cable → Positive (+)** socket, twist clockwise to lock. (DCEP.)
- Leave wire feed power disconnected. Plug the 120V/240V cord into Power Input (not the wall yet).

## Machine settings (p.32)
1. Power OFF → plug into grounded GFCI 120V (20A) / 240V outlet. Electrode holder on a non-conductive, nonflammable surface.
2. Power ON → Home → Main Control Knob to **Stick** → select.
3. Polarity screen ("No Gas Required"): plug cables per screen.
4. **Electrode type screen:** Main Control Knob selects the electrode class — e.g. **60xx or 70xx** (the weld screen shows e.g. "70XX").
5. **Diameter & thickness:** Left Knob = electrode diameter (e.g. 3/32"), Right Knob = material thickness (e.g. 16 Ga).
6. Auto Weld screen: Left Knob = output amperage; **Right Knob = ON energizes the electrode holder** (OCV present — warning).
7. Optional settings (p.33): **Hot Start** (amperage boost at weld start), **Arc Force** (adjusts penetration and smoothness), Recall/Save Setting (5 slots).

## Welding (p.33)
1. Clamp the bare-metal end of the electrode in the holder jaws.
2. Ignite by stroking the workpiece lightly — tap it, stroke it, or **strike it like a match**.
3. Once the arc ignites: lift the electrode off the work **the same distance as the diameter of the bare metal end**, tilt it back **10–20°**, and drag toward the back of the weld puddle, depositing material as needed.
4. Finish: lift the electrode from the work; set the holder on a non-conductive, nonflammable surface; power OFF.
5. After use: cool, unplug, remove ground clamp, disconnect holder and ground cables, store clean and dry out of children's reach.

## Notes
- **Stick welds are covered in slag** — chip it off with the chipping hammer and wire-brush before judging the weld ("CLEAN WELDS FIRST!", p.38; cleaning, p.34).
- Never weld without the grounding clamp; when not holding the electrode holder it must sit on a non-conductive, nonflammable surface. [p.32]
- Duty cycle (p.29): 120V 40% @ 80A (100% @ 60A); 240V 25% @ 175A (100% @ 100A).
- Do the **Strike Test** (p.34) on practice welds.
